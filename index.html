Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about mobile spyware

Why does Certo AntiSpy for iPhone run on a computer instead of an iOS app?
Apple’s iOS security model ("sandboxing") prevents any App Store app from scanning other apps or system files on an iPhone. Any iOS app claiming to scan your iPhone for spyware directly on the phone cannot access deep system directories. By connecting your iPhone to a PC or Mac, Certo AntiSpy performs a true forensic inspection of the entire device backup and system logs.
Will the person spying on my phone know that I ran a Certo scan?
No. Certo’s scanning process is 100% silent and offline-safe. Running a scan will never send an alert or notification to the person who installed stalkerware on your device, allowing you to gather forensic evidence or remove the threat safely.
Can Certo detect commercial stalkerware like mSpy, FlexiSPY, and hidden keyloggers?
Yes. Our threat intelligence lab continuously reverse-engineers the latest versions of mSpy, FlexiSPY, Cocospy, Hoverwatch, uMobix, and custom keyloggers—even when they disguise themselves under system names like "Settings Helper" or hide their app icon.
Is my personal data, photos, or messages uploaded to Certo servers?
Never. Your privacy is our core mission. All scans happen locally on your device or computer. None of your photos, messages, contacts, or personal files ever leave your machine.
